Welcome to MedAgent 2026

The 2nd Workshop on Agentic AI for Medicine, held with MICCAI 2026 in Strasbourg.

Agentic AI is emerging as a new paradigm in medical AI, following the eras of predictive AI and, more recently, generative AI. Unlike earlier systems that run predefined functions or produce outputs passively, agentic AI systems perceive, plan, act and reflect, with large foundation models as their cognitive core. They integrate domain tools, reason over clinical guidelines, manage longitudinal patient data and act within simulated or physical environments.

Building on its first edition at MICCAI 2025 in Daejeon, the second workshop brought together the medical imaging, clinical AI, robotics and healthcare communities. Submissions nearly tripled, from 17 to 46. The morning programme in Strasbourg featured five keynotes, six oral talks and 33 posters on clinical reasoning, imaging agents, tool use, evaluation and governance.

Thank you to our speakers, authors, 29 reviewers and everyone who filled the room. The third edition will follow at MICCAI 2027.
